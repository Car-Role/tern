# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One developer who works across many git repos at once, for work (Azure DevOps) and personal projects (GitHub). Much of the code is written by AI agents (Claude Code) in worktrees. The user's job in Tern is to watch what the agents change, review diffs and pull requests, check CI, pull and push, and hand exact file paths back to agents. Long sessions on a Windows desktop, mostly with the keyboard.

## Product Purpose

A very light, fast, keyboard-first viewer for reviewing many git projects. It is not a full editor. Success means: the user sees what changed across all projects at a glance, reviews it quickly, and gives agents precise context without leaving the app.

## Brand Personality

Fast, quiet, precise. The interface stays out of the way and feels instant. Polish like Linear and Raycast: soft, calm surfaces, clear hierarchy, small careful details, and smooth but short motion.

## Anti-references

- Heavy IDE chrome with many toolbars and panels.
- Generic Electron SaaS: big rounded cards, gradients, glassy panels, marketing-style headers.
- The plain white Windows title bar that breaks the theme.

## Design Principles

1. Speed is the feature: nothing may feel slow, and nothing blocks the keyboard.
2. Dense but calm: show a lot of information, with quiet chrome and one accent.
3. Every surface earns its place: no decoration that does not help a review.
4. Copy is plain: short, exact words; real names, numbers, and commands.
5. Themes are first-class: every part follows the chosen theme, light or dark.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special requirements stated. Keep text contrast readable on every theme, keep full keyboard use, and respect reduced motion.
